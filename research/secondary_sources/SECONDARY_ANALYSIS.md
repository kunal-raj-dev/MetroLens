## 3. Precedential Insights on Enforcement Standards

1. **Strict Liability of Declarations:** High Court precedents under the Legal Metrology Act consistently hold that Section 18 declarations are strict statutory obligations; lack of criminal intent is not a defense against technical non-compliance.
2. **Prominence & Conspicuousness:** Courts have held that declarations must be placed in a prominent position with contrasting background color, validating Nirikshak's planned contrast and readability checks.
3. **Manufacture Date Primacy:** The statutory date is the date of manufacture or pre-packing, reinforcing Nirikshak's non-retroactivity engine to avoid penalizing packages packed before an amendment came into force.
