# Research Directory & Quarantine Policy

## Purpose
Archives exploratory research, academic papers, competitive analysis, participant case studies, and research reports (such as `ch-deep-research-report.md`).

## Non-Negotiable Quarantine Policy
1. **Level 5 Discovery Only:** All content stored in `research/` belongs to Level 3, Level 4, or Level 5 of the project source hierarchy.
2. **Never Legal Authority:** Research notes, summaries, AI outputs, and web scrapes stored here can NEVER be cited as primary legal authority.
3. **No Automatic Rules:** No substantive rule may be committed to `rules/` citing a research report. Only authenticated primary instruments registered in `regulations/source_registry.yaml` may be used as legal authority.
4. **No Implementation Proof:** Research claims or competitor feature matrices do not constitute proof of implementation for Nirikshak.

---

## Directory Organization

```
research/
├── official_sources/    # Secondary research notes on government initiatives
├── secondary_sources/   # Industry articles, market surveys, trade press
├── academic_papers/     # Downloaded PDFs and summaries of peer-reviewed papers
├── prior_art/           # Literature analyses of commercial and open-source tools
├── competitors/         # Publicly available feature comparisons and patents
├── hackathon_winners/   # Analysis of past SIH winning approaches (discovery input only)
└── research_notes/      # Working team notes and exploratory memos
```
