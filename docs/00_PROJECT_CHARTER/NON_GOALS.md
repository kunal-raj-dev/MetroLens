# Non-Goals & Out-of-Scope Declarations

## Purpose
Explicitly outlines technical capabilities, operational tasks, and legal adjudications that Nirikshak will deliberately NOT attempt or claim to perform.

## Scope
Universal across system design, code generation, presentation decks, and judge interactions.

## Authoritative Inputs
- Legal Metrology Act, 2009 statutory officer authority boundaries.
- Project Anti-Hallucination Policy.

## Assumptions
- Clear non-goals protect the team from technical overreach and defend against skeptical judge inquiries.

## Open Questions
- None.

## Dependencies
- `docs/16_LIMITATIONS/`

## Verification Requirements
- Team members must not present any non-goal as a current or near-term capability during live evaluations.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals

1. **Autonomous Legal Prosecution or Compounding:**
   - Nirikshak will **NEVER** issue legal compounding notices, assess monetary fines, or file charge-sheets automatically. All statutory actions require the independent judgment and physical signature of an authorized officer.

2. **Uncalibrated Physical Measurement:**
   - The system will **NEVER** claim to measure font height or package area in millimetres from an uncalibrated arbitrary phone camera snapshot. Uncalibrated captures route strictly to `REVIEW`.

3. **Fake Government Integrations:**
   - The system will **NEVER** simulate or mock fake "official live government APIs" (e.g. fake DoCA or e-Daakhil databases) that have not been provided or verified via official SIH channels.

4. **Microservice Overengineering:**
   - The system will **NEVER** deploy an overcomplicated cluster of 15 microservices, Kafka brokers, or blockchain ledgers. A lean, robust, reproducible architecture (UI $\rightarrow$ API $\rightarrow$ Pipeline $\rightarrow$ DB) is strictly adhered to.

5. **LLM-Based Legal Adjudication:**
   - The system will **NEVER** ask a Large Language Model (LLM) to decide whether a package is legally compliant. Compliance decisions are executed exclusively by deterministic code evaluated against verified rule schemas.

6. **Automated Evidence Admissibility Claims:**
   - The system will **NEVER** claim that its PDF dossiers are automatically admissible under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 without formal forensic and procedural compliance by the presenting officer.
