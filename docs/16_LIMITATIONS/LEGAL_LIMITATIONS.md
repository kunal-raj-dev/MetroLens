# Legal Limitations & Non-Adjudicative Boundaries

## Purpose
Establishes the explicit legal boundaries, statutory disclaimers, and limits of authority of the Nirikshak system.

## Core Boundaries
1. **Decision Support Only:** The system assists authorized enforcement officers. It does not possess independent legal personality or statutory enforcement powers under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
2. **No Automated Penalties:** The system will never automatically levy fines, issue compounding orders, or launch criminal prosecutions.
3. **Court Admissibility Determination:** Whether generated PDF dossiers and cryptographic records are admitted as evidence is the sole prerogative of the competent judicial forum under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
4. **Primary Source Supremacy:** Machine-readable rules in `rules/` are engineering models. In any legal dispute, the official Gazette of India publication remains the exclusive sovereign authority.
