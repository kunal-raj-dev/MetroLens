# Government Legal Metrology & E-Governance Systems

## Purpose
Catalogues existing government IT systems, consumer portals, and state enforcement databases to identify integration points and boundary limits.

## Systems Reviewed
1. **e-Daakhil Portal:** National online consumer grievance redressal platform.
2. **National Consumer Helpline (NCH):** DoCA complaint registry.
3. **State Legal Metrology Portals:** License verification and fee collection portals across various Indian states.
4. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Care App:** Mobile verification of ISI marks and hallmark registration.

> [!NOTE]
> Nirikshak does not simulate or claim unverified live integrations with these portals. Future integration points are documented under `docs/13_BUILD_PLAN/ROADMAP.md`.
