# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Regulatory Changelog

## Purpose
Provides an auditable chronological timeline of official amendments to the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011.

## Scope
Tracks gazetted amendments, commencement dates, affected rules, and supersession relationships.

## Authoritative Inputs
- The Gazette of India notifications (DoCA series).
- `regulations/source_registry.yaml`.

## Assumptions
- An amendment published in the Gazette takes effect on its explicitly stated commencement date, not necessarily its publication date.

## Chronological Amendment Record

### 1. Base Enactment (2011)
- **Instrument:** The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
- **Notification:** G.S.R. 202(E) dated 7th March 2011.
- **Published:** 2011-03-07 | **Commenced:** 2011-04-01.
- **Source ID:** `IN-LMPC-2011-GSR202E`.
- **Key Provisions:** Base framework for retail/wholesale packages, Rule 6 mandatory declarations, Rule 7 area of PDP and Table-I minimum numeral heights.

### 2. Major Amendment (2017)
- **Instrument:** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2017.
- **Notification:** G.S.R. 629(E) dated 23rd June 2017.
- **Published:** 2017-06-23 | **Commenced:** 2018-01-01.
- **Source ID:** `IN-LMPC-2017-GSR629E`.
- **Key Provisions:** Mandated declarations on e-commerce marketplaces; enhanced font size visibility requirements; prohibited dual MRP.

### 3. Unit Sale Price & Standard Quantities Amendment (2021/2022)
- **Instrument:** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2021.
- **Notification:** G.S.R. 779(E) dated 2nd November 2021.
- **Published:** 2021-11-02 | **Commenced:** 2022-12-01 (postponed from original schedule via transitional orders).
- **Source ID:** `IN-LMPC-2021-GSR779E`.
- **Key Provisions:** Mandatory Unit Sale Price (USP) declaration on all pre-packaged commodities; removal of Schedule II mandatory package quantity restrictions for certain commodities.

### 4. Proposed Amendments (2024 / 2026 Consultation)
- **Instrument:** Draft Amendment to Rule 3 of LMPC Rules.
- **Status:** `PROPOSED` / `DRAFT` (NOT CURRENT LAW).
- **Source ID:** `IN-LMPC-2024-DRAFT-R3`.
- **Engineering Policy:** Under no circumstances are draft proposals applied to live compliance inspections.
