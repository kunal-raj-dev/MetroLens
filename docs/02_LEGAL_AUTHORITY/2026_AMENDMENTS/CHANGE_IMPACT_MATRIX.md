# 2026 Regulatory Amendments & Proposals Impact Matrix

## Purpose
Evaluates emerging gazette notifications, recent amendments, and draft proposals published by DoCA to assess their engineering and compliance impact.

## Scope
Covers recent gazettes (such as G.S.R. 128(E), G.S.R. 312(E), G.S.R. 418(E) if gazetted) and draft consultation instruments.

## Authoritative Inputs
- Official Gazette of India notifications.
- Department of Consumer Affairs official draft notices.

## Assumptions
- Draft proposals do NOT alter in-force compliance rules until officially gazetted with a declared commencement date.
