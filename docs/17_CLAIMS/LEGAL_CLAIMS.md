# Legal Claims Policy & Register

## Purpose
Establishes clear boundaries around legal interpretations, statutory authority claims, and compliance claims.

## Core Rules
1. Nirikshak does NOT determine legal guilt, liability, or prosecutorial outcomes.
2. Reports are technical inspection assistance dossiers; whether they carry legal evidentiary weight is subject to competent court determination.
3. Every machine-readable rule cites an exact primary source record in `regulations/source_registry.yaml`.
