## 1. Executive Summary

This audit evaluates all proposed datasets, external government portals, commercial product registries, and third-party APIs considered during the architecture and planning phases of Nirikshak (SIH 2026 — PS 26034).

### Key Audit Findings:
1. **Offline-First Statutory Resilience:** Nirikshak is designed to perform its optical capture, OCR extraction, geometric calibration, and declarative rule verification **entirely on-device without an active internet connection**. It does not require any real-time external API calls to issue an inspection finding.
2. **Prohibition of Web Scraping:** Unauthenticated or mass scraping of commercial e-commerce websites (e.g., Amazon, Blinkit, Zepto) or search engines is strictly **`REJECTED — PROHIBITED BY POLICY`** due to terms of service violations, lack of chain-of-custody, and copyright liability.
3. **External Portals & Integrations:** Portals such as e-Daakhil, National Consumer Helpline (NCH), and GS1 India DataKart are cataloged as optional post-inspection export targets or secondary cross-reference lookups (`PLANNED` / `PRIMARY_SOURCE_REQUIRED`), with no dependency for core inspection workflows.
4. **Data Privacy & Officer Protection:** Digital evidence packages generated by Nirikshak contain only statutory packaging disclosures and officer inspection metadata; no biometric or unauthorized consumer PII is captured or persisted.

## 4. Privacy, Security & Compliance Analysis

### 4.1 Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance
- **No Consumer PII Collection:** The Nirikshak capture interface strictly targets the physical product packaging. No consumer faces, personal identity cards, or private consumer data are captured or processed.
- **Inspector Credentials:** Officer usernames and badge numbers recorded in the audit trail are cryptographically hashed and governed by role-based access control (`RBAC`).
- **Geo-Location Tagging:** Inspection GPS coordinates are recorded solely to establish legal jurisdiction and local enforcement venue (District Legal Metrology Office) under Section 15 of the Act, with explicit officer confirmation.

### 4.2 Offline Operational Guarantee
Statutory packaging inspections occur in warehouses, retail mandi stalls, factory dispatch bays, and remote rural distributor locations where cellular network availability is unreliable or nonexistent.
- **Local Engine Execution:** PaddleOCR/Tesseract models, image processing filters, and the declarative rule engine execute completely on the local edge hardware (laptop or field tablet).
- **Zero Cloud Leakage:** Inspection images and reports are stored in local encrypted SQLite/PostgreSQL databases; no image data is transmitted to third-party cloud APIs (such as OpenAI, Google Vision API, or AWS Rekognition) for OCR or analysis.

## 5. Audit Conclusions & Recommendations

1. **Strict Offline Purity:** Nirikshak's architecture maintains complete independence from external APIs during the active inspection lifecycle.
2. **Clear Licensing Separation:** All datasets must have verified provenance before inclusion in Stage 2 empirical benchmarks.
3. **No Web Scraping:** The blanket ban on automated commercial web scraping remains enforced and verified across all project documentation.
