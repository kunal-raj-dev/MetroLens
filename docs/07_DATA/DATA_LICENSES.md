# Dataset Licensing & Permitted Usage

## Purpose
Specifies the copyright licenses, distribution rights, and commercial reuse restrictions governing datasets compiled for Nirikshak.

## Scope
Applies to all data assets in `data/`, `benchmarks/datasets/`, and `assets/sample_packages/`.

## Authoritative Inputs
- `docs/DATA_LICENSES.md`
- Creative Commons (CC) licensing framework.

## Assumptions
- Synthetic data generated by the project is released under public domain / CC0 terms.

## Open Questions
- **OQ-RIGHTS-01:** Scope of statutory exemption under Section 52(1)(a) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 for photographing commercial retail packaging in computational vision research [status: RIGHTS_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED].
- **OQ-RIGHTS-02:** Formal copyright assignment protocol for bounding-box and text-transcription ground-truth annotations created by student team members [status: RIGHTS_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED].
- **OQ-RIGHTS-03:** Trade dress infringement risk assessment under Trade Marks Act, 1999 for publishing retail packaging datasets in open-source repositories [status: RESTRICTED].

## Dependencies
- `data/manifests/manifest.yaml`

## Verification Requirements
- Manifest verification script `scripts/verification/verify_dataset_manifest.py` checks that every dataset has a declared license.
